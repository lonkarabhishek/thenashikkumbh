# Two-minute demo script

**Audience:** NTKMA officials, Divisional Commissioner Office, Nashik.
**Setup:** Phone, in-browser, mobile viewport, network on for the first 90 seconds.

---

## 0:00 — Landing

Open `https://thenashikkumbh.com` on a phone.

> "This is the pilgrim's view. There are three things at the top — ask the assistant, hear a story, see the sacred dates. Everything below is grouped by what a pilgrim does: before, arriving, inside, emergency."

## 0:15 — Verified dates

Tap **Sacred Dates**.

> "These are the three Amrit Snans and the Dhwajarohan from the DGIPR release on 21 May 2025. Every date is tagged with its source. If NTKMA revises a date, one file changes on our side and every page updates."

## 0:35 — Assistant with source

Tap the assistant. Ask: **"When is the first Shahi Snan?"**

> "The answer carries a source, the source organisation, and a verified date, right under the reply. Now watch a safety question."

Ask: **"What do I do in a stampede?"**

> "The assistant refuses to invent an answer, tells the user to dial 112, and gives them the NTKMA numbers. It never gambles with life-safety."

## 1:00 — Offline emergency

Turn on Airplane Mode. Open the app icon or the `/emergency` link.

> "The service worker keeps the emergency card and the 112 button available with no data. That is the one thing on this site that must never fail."

## 1:20 — Governance

Turn network back on. Open `/changelog` and `/policies`.

> "Every factual correction we have made since launch is logged publicly. Editorial rules and privacy are in plain language. Nothing is hidden."

## 1:45 — Handover

> "The site is independent today. If NTKMA wants it under an official domain, the code is portable, the data model is documented, and I will support the handover. If NTKMA wants a data feed instead, we can consume it and the operational content updates automatically."

## 2:00 — Close

> "The ask is simple — one point of contact and read access to whatever data NTKMA is willing to share."
