# Source code and data ownership

## Current state

- Source code: personal repository under the maintainer's GitHub account.
- Content: written and curated by the maintainer, sourced against public government releases.
- Domain: `thenashikkumbh.com`, registered by the maintainer.
- Hosting: Vercel, on the maintainer's account.

## Options for NTKMA

### 1. Independent-supplement (current default)

The site continues to run independently. NTKMA is under no obligation. The site remains explicitly labelled an independent public-information initiative, and pilgrims are directed to NTKMA for anything requiring authority.

### 2. Endorsement, no handover

The site continues to be maintained independently, NTKMA reviews and endorses specific pages, and an "endorsed by NTKMA on YYYY-MM-DD" badge appears on those pages. Editorial control stays with the maintainer.

### 3. Fork under a government domain

The maintainer publishes the source under an OSI-approved licence (default: MIT). NTKMA forks the codebase, hosts it on a `*.gov.in` domain, and runs its own editorial process. The maintainer commits to answering technical questions for 90 days after handover, at no charge.

### 4. Full transfer

The maintainer transfers the domain, repository, content, and hosting to NTKMA. NTKMA takes full ownership. The maintainer commits to a 90-day support window at no charge.

## What NTKMA does not need to do

- Pay for the platform to exist.
- Take on any liability for the site as long as it is not endorsed.
- Adopt any specific technology stack.

## What NTKMA is asked to do, in any option

- If the site publishes a fact NTKMA believes to be wrong, tell the maintainer. Corrections are actioned within 24 hours and logged publicly.
