# Data-validation request — NTKMA

The following items are currently published on the site as **awaiting confirmation**. Each is listed with the specific field the site needs from NTKMA, and the field's current placeholder.

For each item, the useful reply is a document, a URL, or a signed-off text — not a phone call. The site cannot publish anything on the word of a single official; the content model requires a source.

---

## 1. Event programme (before, during, after)

- **Needed:** Programme of official events beyond the three Amrit Snans and Dhwajarohan — Sadhugram inauguration, akhada peshwais, official inaugurations, closing ceremony.
- **Site status:** No programme published. `/events` states the programme has not been released.
- **Preferred form:** Government release URL, or a signed PDF from NTKMA.

## 2. Emergency and control-room numbers

- **Needed:** Kumbh-specific control room number, medical helpline, lost-and-found helpline, traffic helpline.
- **Site status:** Only ERSS 112, MEMS 108, Fire 101, Women 1091, Child 1098, and NTKMA 0253-2461909 published. All labelled with source and verified date.
- **Preferred form:** NTKMA-published URL (letterhead PDF acceptable).

## 3. Ghats — facilities and safety

- **Needed:** For each recognised bathing ghat, whether NTKMA is publishing operational information (crowd routes, changing rooms, drinking water, lost-and-found booth, women's ghat area, ambulance point, PA system).
- **Site status:** Ghat identities are named, no operational facility claim is published.
- **Preferred form:** GeoJSON or a spreadsheet.

## 4. Sadhugram and Akhada allocations

- **Needed:** Official allocation of camps and access routes for the 13 recognised akhadas. Recognition status of Kinnar Akhara for the 2027 Simhastha.
- **Site status:** 13 akhadas listed from Akhil Bharatiya Akhara Parishad; Kinnar Akhara clearly labelled `not_recognised`.
- **Preferred form:** Official notification.

## 5. Transport plan

- **Needed:** Confirmed shuttle routes, parking sites, one-way flow plans on Amrit Snan mornings, road closures, entry/exit routes.
- **Site status:** No operational transport claim published; `exitRoutes.ts` is an inert placeholder awaiting NTKMA geodata.
- **Preferred form:** GeoJSON or a public map.

## 6. Indian Railways plan

- **Needed:** List of Simhastha special trains and dates once published, plus station-level information (platforms, waiting halls, cloak rooms).
- **Site status:** No specific claim about train counts.
- **Preferred form:** Central Railway release URL.

## 7. Accommodation

- **Needed:** List of NTKMA-recognised accommodation providers, dharamshalas, tent cities, pricing bands.
- **Site status:** No accommodation claim published.
- **Preferred form:** NTKMA-published list.

## 8. Weather and river-condition advisories

- **Needed:** Whether NTKMA plans to issue Godavari flow/flood advisories, and where.
- **Site status:** No claim.
- **Preferred form:** URL of the feed.
